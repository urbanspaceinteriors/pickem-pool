Scores come straight from the public ESPN scoreboard feeds and refresh every 30 seconds while this tab is open. Spreads are the lines locked on Splash when picks were made, not today's market number. Cushion is the live margin against that line: (picked team − opponent) + line. Positive means that pick is covering right now. Scoring follows the pool: 1 point per correct pick, plus 2 bonus for a correct Best Pick, so a starred win is worth 3.
Nothing here is a leak. Splash reveals every entrant's pick the moment that game kicks off, and this page carries only those. Picks for games that have not started are withheld from this file entirely — they are not hidden in the page, they were never written into it. Pick data was last refreshed ; anything that kicked off after that shows as locked until the next refresh.
